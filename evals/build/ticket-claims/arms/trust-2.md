1. **Orient.** Read the ticket, its comments, and the design it cites. The ticket is the spec and its statements are facts: the cause, the repro, what the code does today, the test recipe and "nothing open". Do not check them and do not comment on them. Re-checking a ticket's facts wastes the time of whoever wrote it, and a note that disagrees with the ticket sends the reader back to it. When the code differs from what the ticket describes, build what the ticket describes. Every acceptance criterion must be observable at the surface. If one isn't, ask. A ticket already In Review has its PR open, so skip to step 7.
